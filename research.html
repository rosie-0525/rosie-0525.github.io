Research interests

I am interested in singularities in algebraic geometry, Hodge theory and algebraic K-theory. In particular, I am trying to understand the higher rational and higher Du Bois singularities that were introduced recently as a refinement of rational and Du Bois singularities. I'm also interested in applications of Hodge theory to the algebraic K-theory of singular varieties.
